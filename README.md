# StoryStream 🎧

A free audiobook player for thousands of **public-domain** audiobooks recorded by the volunteers at [LibriVox](https://librivox.org). Made by Galaxy.

**Open it:** https://oscarplaysroblox.github.io/storystream/

- Search by book title or author, or browse Popular, Kids, Adventure, Fantasy, Fairy Tales, Mystery, Sci-Fi, and more
- Book pages with cover, description, and chapter list
- Player with play/pause, back 15s / forward 30s, next/previous chapter, speed (0.75× to 2×), and a sleep timer
- Remembers where you stopped in every book (saved only on your device), with Continue listening and My Library
- Works on iPhone, iPad and Windows. On iPhone/iPad, open it in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**, and it works like an app with lock-screen controls

## Where the books come from
All recordings are by LibriVox volunteers and hosted by the [Internet Archive](https://archive.org/details/librivoxaudio) (`collection:librivoxaudio`). The app talks directly to the Internet Archive's public search and metadata APIs. Every book is in the public domain. No ads, no trackers, no accounts.
